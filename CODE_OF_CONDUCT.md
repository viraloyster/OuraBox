# Code of conduct

OuraBox exists so that people with ideas outside their lane have a safe place to work on them. The project's own spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) have to be safe in the same way.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, whatever their background, degrees, experience, origin, gender, age or beliefs.
- Criticize ideas and code, never the person. Honest is fine; cruel is not.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Help newcomers: everyone started without knowing.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or sexual or discriminatory language.
- Mocking someone's idea, level or way of writing.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Insisting after being asked to stop.

## If something happens

Write to the maintainer privately (the contact is on the [@viraloyster](https://github.com/viraloyster) profile). Every report is read, kept confidential, and answered. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in any of the project's spaces.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
