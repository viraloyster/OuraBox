Each version of the desktop app, newest first. The installers are on the [Releases](https://github.com/viraloyster/OuraBox/releases) page.

## 0.1.0 (beta)

The first public version of the safe space for PC. [What beta means](https://github.com/viraloyster/OuraBox/blob/main/docs/08-plan.md#what-beta-means).

- **An encrypted space per user**, with a password and a recovery key; lock, sign out and encrypted backups.
- **Projects, files and quick notes**, with a trash that never erases anything right away.
- **Writing and drawing on the same page:** text, free text boxes, tables, images, shapes and freehand ink, with one undo for all of it.
- **Roots:** go deeper from any phrase, and see the whole tree on the roots map.
- **About this idea:** what you want to achieve, a single next step and the assumptions you take for granted.
- **Up to four documents side by side**, search across everything, export to PDF, image or text.
- **Skies** (themes) and three languages: English, Español and Português (Brasil).
