# Philosophy

## The problem it solves

When a naive idea collides with the real world, the world is not usually fair to it: it either mocks it ("that's impossible") or ignores it. That collision can kill two things at once:

1. **The idea**, which perhaps needed to transform.
2. **The person's desire to have the next idea**, which should never have died.

Also, the work needed to move an idea forward is often so much that it overwhelms, and the idea stays still.

## The box

Everything we know, including the laws of physics, works **under assumptions**. Those assumptions are the box: what we call *in the box*.

OuraBox doesn't invite people to break ideas. It invites them to **see and question the assumptions**, and gives them the space to work on it. That is how knowledge has always advanced: Euclid's geometry was "the truth" for 2,000 years until someone questioned the parallel postulate; Newton was "the law" until Einstein questioned that time is the same for everyone.

## Principles

1. **Naivety is raw material, not a mistake.** OuraBox isn't here to take it away from you. It's here to give it a place to work.

2. **Cold but warm.** It's a balance: honest about the idea and warm toward the person. It neither inflates nor crushes.

3. **It doesn't give verdicts: it shows the box.** It speaks of the current state of knowledge ("so far it has not been shown…"), not of eternal sentences, and it makes visible the assumptions each obstacle rests on.

4. **It doesn't put people in the box.** It doesn't choose the path or offer a closed menu of options. It presents the challenge and **hands the question back**: the one who thinks outside the box is the one who finds the way out.

5. **It offers help, it doesn't impose it.** "If you want, we can…": help is an option, not a route.

6. **It asks, it doesn't interpret.** "I think you're looking for…" instead of "what you want is…".

7. **It recognizes ambition, it doesn't inflate the ego.** "An ambitious project" speaks of the size of what the person dares to attempt, not of whether the idea is good.

8. **Hope, not burden.** People should leave OuraBox wanting to keep going, not carrying more weight.

9. **Ideas are private by default.** Showing them is always the decision of the person who had them.

10. **Each idea at its own pace.** There are quick ideas and deep ideas, and the space has to serve both.

11. **The field is secondary.** Programming, agriculture, sports or art: the journey from the abstract to the executable is the same.
