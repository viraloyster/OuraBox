# Open questions

What is still unresolved. When a question is answered, the answer moves to the document it belongs in.

## Safe space

- How is a quick idea captured without friction?
- How does a deep idea look, and how is it worked on?
- How do you "show the box" for an idea that isn't technical (a business, a work of art, a way of organizing something)?
- When does the AI appear, and when does it stay quiet?
- Is the definition of "private" proposed in [Technology](07-technology.md) the right one?
- Do we need to be able to generate a new recovery key?
- What is the criterion for considering stage 1 validated?
- Is the concept validated with a simple visual prototype before building the secure core in Rust?
- Which of the features proposed in [The safe space](09-safe-space.md) go into stage 1?
- What will the sections finally be called? Direct names that don't confuse.
- How often does OuraBox notify, and who chooses?
- How will tools like Word, Excel or Canva be integrated later on?

## Design

- How do we make the difference from other tools for ideas and notes noticeable?
- Do we add the option for the sky to follow the time of day (morning, noon, sunset, night)? If added, the change must never happen abruptly while someone is working.
- Does the frosted glass meet the performance budget on the minimum hardware?

## Community

- When and how does an idea move from the safe space to the community?
- What shape does it take: continuous work, seasons, challenges?
- How does it stay healthy and true to the philosophy as it grows?

## Companies

- How exactly does the commission from companies work?
- How are people and their work protected?
- How do we keep monetization from corrupting the other two spaces?

## General

- Who decides what "the current state of knowledge" is when OuraBox responds?
- When and where is the OuraBox trademark (name and logo) registered? The code already has a license (see [Technology](07-technology.md), T12), but the license doesn't protect the name.
- How are donations organized?
- AI and sync cost money. If users aren't charged, how are they funded?
- How much Rust experience does the person building it have, and how much external review will be needed?
