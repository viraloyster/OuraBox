# Vision

## The name

**OuraBox** comes from **Out of the Box**. That alone says almost everything.

## The phrase that inspires it

> *"Hay gente que juega mejor que el Bayern y no tiene para comer."*
> *(There are people who play better than Bayern and don't have enough to eat.)*

It's not about football; it's a metaphor. There are people with enormous talent, creative, structured, with different points of view, who never get to play in the top division because nobody sees them. OuraBox exists to give them a place to work, grow and be seen, and so they play on our team.

The phrase speaks of **talent without a showcase**, not only of a lack of money. OuraBox's mission includes people with few resources, but it is not limited to them.

## In one sentence

> **A safe space to develop genuine ideas.**

## Who it's for

People with ideas and callings that live outside their official lane. For example:

- The lawyer who wanted to be an architect since childhood.
- The programmer who always wanted to be a musical artist.
- Young people with amazing ideas and full of naivety. It's not an insult: naivety is exactly what lets you see what others have stopped seeing.
- Someone who has good ideas but hasn't been able to move forward with them, because the work they involve is too much and nobody sees their potential.
- People with few resources and talent that nobody sees.
- Anyone, no matter who they are or what degrees they hold. What matters is **what they bring and what they work on**.

There are countless examples, and not all of them are people with few resources.

This project comes from that experience: the person who created it is, still, one of those people.

## The central idea

> **The world is cruel and people are born naive.**
> **OuraBox is the portal that carries naivety into the real world without the clash of worlds.**

It is a portal from abstract ideas to the world of execution, whatever the field: programming, agriculture, sports or any other. The field is secondary.

## What OuraBox is not

- **It's not a "GitHub 2.0".** It's not just a technical place to store work; it aims to be something psychologically stronger and healthier.
- **It's not "tell me the right phrase and I'll hire you".** It's not a recruiting filter in disguise.
- **It doesn't inflate egos.** It doesn't say "what an incredible idea, you're the best!".
- **It doesn't put people in the box.** It doesn't use psychological tricks to steer people who think differently back toward the usual.
- **It doesn't overwhelm.** Its goal is to give hope, real hope and not empty praise.
- **It doesn't charge users.** See [Economic model](11-economic-model.md).

## How far it wants to go

OuraBox has three spaces (see [The three spaces](04-spaces.md)): the safe space, the community and companies. When it grows enough, the ambition is to open **remote worldwide events** on the platform, where talent from anywhere in the world can show itself.
