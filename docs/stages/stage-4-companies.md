# Stage 4: Companies

> **Status: idea, not started.** Nothing here is built, and it may never be. If it goes ahead, it would be planned in detail once a community exists and works.

## Goal

That the talent working in OuraBox finds open doors at companies, and that OuraBox sustains itself financially.

## What is thought so far

From what we learned from others (see [Lessons](../05-lessons.md)):

- **Ideas are not sold to companies.** Companies avoid receiving outside ideas because of legal risk, and a standalone idea has almost no market value.
- The space revolves around **people and work**.
- **The person with the idea is never charged in exchange for hope.**
- **Users are not charged.** One option is for OuraBox to earn a commission from companies (see [Economic model](../11-economic-model.md)).
- If OuraBox wins even when people don't do well, the model is broken.

## Questions to answer before starting

- How exactly does the commission work?
- How are people and their work protected from companies?
- How do we keep monetization from corrupting the safe space and the community?
- How does OuraBox sustain itself before reaching this stage? Donations are one option.
