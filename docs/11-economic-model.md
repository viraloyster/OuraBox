# Economic model

How OuraBox sustains itself.

Legend: **Decided** means agreed; **Option** means considered but not decided; **Proposed** means recommended but still to be confirmed.

## Decided

- **Users are not charged, in any direct way.**

## Options

- **Donations**, as a possible way to start.
- **A commission from companies**, if the companies space (stage 4) is ever built. Never from users.

## Why it's viable in stage 1

In stage 1 everything lives on each person's PC and there's no server (see [Technology](07-technology.md)). That's why the cost of maintaining OuraBox doesn't grow with each new user.

The costs appear later:

- **Sync** between devices (stage 2, if it happens): it requires a server.
- **AI:** each use has a cost.

## A precedent

**Blender**, the 3D program, is free and open source. Its development is funded by a fund that receives donations from individuals and contributions from companies that use it. It's an example of a tool that's free for everyone, sustained by those who benefit from it.

## Proposed

- **Only the person decides when to show themselves to a company.** If OuraBox earns a commission when someone is hired, there's a temptation to push people toward companies. The rule avoids that conflict: OuraBox never earns at the person's expense.

## Pending
