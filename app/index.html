    Language

    Security

    Your ideas are saved encrypted. Here you decide how they are protected.

    Users on this computer

    Each person signs in with their username and password to their own encrypted space. No one can see the others' spaces, or which users there are: to sign in you have to type the name.

    Auto-lock

    If you don't use OuraBox for this long, it locks itself.

    Encrypted backup

    A file with your whole space, encrypted. Keep it wherever you like, such as a USB drive or Google Drive: without your password it can't be read. To use it on another computer, open it when you create your space there.

    Change password

    Your documents don't change; only the password that opens them. Your recovery key still works.

    Recovery key

    If you lost your key or want a new one, create another. The old one stops opening this space; the backups you already made still open with the key they had.
